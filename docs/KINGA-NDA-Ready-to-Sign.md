## 1. Purpose

The Individual will have access to the Company's confidential information, source code, claim data, and business materials in connection with their work for the Company. This Agreement sets out the obligations that apply to that access.

---

## 2. Confidential Information

**2.1** "**Confidential Information**" means all information, in any form, that relates to the Company, its technology, operations, or clients, and that is disclosed to or accessed by the Individual in the course of their engagement. This includes, without limitation:

**(a) Technical assets** — source code, compiled binaries, configuration files, database schemas, infrastructure code, system architecture, design documents, API specifications, data models, the KINGA Engineering Manual, and all technical documentation for the KINGA platform and any related systems;

**(b) Fraud-scoring and forensic methodology** — fraud-scoring logic, scoring weights, band thresholds, and all parameters used to classify claims by risk level; physics calibration constants, vehicle stiffness coefficients, crush depth methodology, speed inference method weights, consensus algorithm parameters, and the forensic reconstruction methodology used in the KINGA physics pipeline; any information that would allow a third party to reverse-engineer, circumvent, or predict the outputs of these systems;

**(c) Financial and commercial configuration** — pricing structures, cost configuration, financial thresholds, business rules embedded in the cost optimisation engine, commercial terms with insurers, panel beaters, assessors, or other partners, and all revenue, margin, and financial projection data;

**(d) Claim data and personal information** — all personal information relating to claimants, insured persons, third parties, insurers, panel beaters, or assessors processed through the KINGA platform, including names, contact details, vehicle information, incident descriptions, photographs, and assessment outcomes;

**(e) Business strategy and commercial intelligence** — business strategy, product roadmap (including KINGA Labs and any future divisions), unreleased product plans, client and prospect lists, insurer and partner relationships, and all commercial terms;

**(f) Any other information** that is marked "Confidential" or "Restricted," or that a reasonable person in the Individual's position would understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure. The Parties acknowledge that Confidential Information is frequently disclosed verbally, during onboarding, or via screen-sharing, and the absence of a written label does not affect its confidential character.

**2.2** Confidential Information includes information belonging to third parties (including insurers, claimants, and panel beaters) that the Company holds under a duty of confidentiality.

---

## 3. Obligations

**3.1** The Individual must:

**(a)** keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential and not disclose it to any person outside the Company without the Company's prior written consent;

**(b)** use Confidential Information only for the purpose of performing their work for the Company;

**(c)** not copy, reproduce, or record Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for their work;

**(d)** not input any Confidential Information — including claim data, fraud-scoring parameters, physics calibration values, or financial configuration — into any general-purpose AI tool, large language model, or external processing service that is not part of the Company's approved toolchain;

**(e)** not export, copy, or transmit personal information relating to claimants, insurers, or panel beaters to personal devices, personal cloud storage, personal email accounts, or any unapproved tool or service;

**(f)** report any actual or suspected unauthorised disclosure of Confidential Information to the Company immediately upon becoming aware of it.

**3.2** These obligations apply to all work performed by the Individual, regardless of the device, system, location, or channel used.

---

## 4. Restricted-Tier Materials

**4.1** Certain categories of Confidential Information are subject to heightened restrictions ("**Restricted-Tier Materials**"), being:

- fraud-scoring weights, band thresholds, and all numerical parameters used to classify claims by risk level;
- physics calibration constants, including vehicle stiffness coefficients, crush depth thresholds, speed inference method weights, and consensus algorithm parameters;
- financial configuration, including pricing tiers, cost thresholds, and margin parameters;
- any document marked "Restricted — Internal Use Only."

**4.2** If the Individual is granted access to Restricted-Tier Materials, they must additionally:

**(a)** not reproduce, transcribe, copy, or record any specific numerical value from Restricted-Tier Materials outside Company-controlled systems — this prohibition applies to personal notes (handwritten or digital), personal devices, personal cloud storage, and any communication channel not operated by the Company;

**(b)** not discuss or share specific values from Restricted-Tier Materials in any external channel, including personal messaging applications, personal email, code repositories not operated by the Company, or any third-party AI tool or processing service.

**4.3** The Individual acknowledges that Restricted-Tier Materials are the category of information that most directly determines the Company's competitive position and the integrity of its forensic assessment outputs, and that unauthorised disclosure would cause harm not adequately compensable by damages alone.

---

## 5. Exclusions

The obligations in this Agreement do not apply to information that the Individual can demonstrate, by clear written evidence:

**(a)** was already in the public domain at the time of disclosure through no act or omission of the Individual;

**(b)** subsequently enters the public domain through no act or omission of the Individual;

**(c)** was already known to the Individual before disclosure, as evidenced by records predating the disclosure; or

**(d)** was received from a third party who was lawfully entitled to disclose it without any duty of confidentiality.

If the Individual is required by law or court order to disclose any Confidential Information, they must notify the Company promptly before making any disclosure (to the extent permitted by law) and disclose only the minimum information required.

---

## 6. Intellectual Property

**6.1** All intellectual property rights in all work product created by the Individual in the course of their engagement with the Company — including source code, documentation, designs, analyses, reports, models, algorithms, data structures, and all other materials — vest in and belong to the Company.

**6.2** To the extent that any such intellectual property rights do not automatically vest in the Company by operation of law, the Individual hereby assigns to the Company, with full title guarantee and as a present assignment taking effect immediately on creation, all such intellectual property rights in all work product created in the course of the engagement, including the right to apply for registrations, renewals, and extensions of those rights in any jurisdiction.

**6.3** The Individual must promptly execute all documents and do all acts reasonably required by the Company to give effect to this assignment and to register or enforce the Company's rights in any jurisdiction.

**6.4** This clause does not apply to tools, libraries, or materials created by the Individual entirely before the commencement of the engagement, provided that the Individual discloses any such pre-existing materials to the Company in writing before incorporating them into any work product. Where pre-existing materials are incorporated into work product, the Individual grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use those materials as part of the work product.

**6.5** "**Intellectual property rights**" means all patents, copyright, database rights, trade secrets, know-how, trade marks, design rights, and all other intellectual property rights, whether registered or unregistered, subsisting anywhere in the world.

---

## 7. Data Protection

**7.1** The Individual must comply with all applicable data protection legislation in the jurisdictions where the Company operates, including Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act and Zambia's Data Protection Act, in relation to all personal information processed through the KINGA platform.

**7.2** The Individual must access personal information only to the extent strictly necessary for the performance of their assigned duties, and must comply with any data handling procedures or data classification policies issued by the Company from time to time.

---

## 8. Duration and Survival

**8.1** This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues for the duration of the Individual's engagement with the Company and thereafter as follows:

**(a)** Obligations relating to trade secrets — being the fraud-scoring weights, physics calibration constants, financial configuration, and any other information that qualifies as a trade secret — survive termination of the engagement **indefinitely**.

**(b)** Obligations relating to all other Confidential Information survive termination of the engagement for a period of **three (3) years** from the date of termination.

**(c)** The IP assignment in clause 6 is permanent and does not lapse on termination.

---

## 9. Return of Materials

On the termination of the engagement (for any reason) or on written demand by the Company at any time, the Individual must:

**(a)** immediately return to the Company all Confidential Information and all materials containing Confidential Information in the Individual's possession or control, in whatever form;

**(b)** permanently delete all Confidential Information from personal devices, personal cloud storage accounts, and any other location outside the Company's systems, and confirm such deletion in writing to the Company within five (5) business days of termination;

**(c)** to the extent feasible, delete or request deletion of any Confidential Information from the history, logs, or memory of any AI tool or external service used in the course of the engagement; and

**(d)** cooperate with the Company's IT team to confirm that all system access credentials have been revoked.

---

## 10. Remedies

The Individual acknowledges that a breach of this Agreement — particularly a breach involving Restricted-Tier Materials — may cause the Company irreparable harm that cannot be adequately compensated by damages alone, and that the Company may seek injunctive relief or other equitable remedies in addition to any claim for damages.

---

## 11. Non-Solicitation

During the engagement and for a period of twelve (12) months following its termination, the Individual must not, directly or indirectly, solicit or attempt to solicit any employee or contractor of the Company with whom the Individual worked, for the purpose of inducing them to leave the Company.

---

## 12. General

**12.1 Entire agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior representations, understandings, and agreements relating to confidentiality and intellectual property.

**12.2 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is found to be unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

**12.3 No waiver.** Failure to enforce any provision of this Agreement does not constitute a waiver of the right to enforce it in the future.

**12.4 Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of Zimbabwe. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement that cannot be resolved by negotiation shall be referred to the courts of Zimbabwe.
