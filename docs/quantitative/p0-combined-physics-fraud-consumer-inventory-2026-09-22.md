# P0 Combined Physics-and-Fraud Consumer Inventory

**Date:** 22 September 2026
**Status:** Read-only source inventory completed after P0-A-2 merged in PR #144. This is a scope-control and owner-decision record. It authorizes no implementation, migration, data change, deployment, or P0-B1 merge.
**Baseline:** `ee8b80e4`, the merge commit for P0-A-2. [2]
**Compared overlay:** paused, uncommitted P0-B1 worktree based on pre-A2 commit `a18d692`.

## Conclusion

> **P0-A-3 and P0-B1c are not the final groups.** The audit found 40 canonical consumer groups: 32 material decision or publication groups, six raw-evidence-preservation groups, and two test or dormant-reference groups. Of the 32 material groups, only seven have a confirmed physics-safe branch in merged P0-A-2. Thirteen have prospective fraud protection in P0-B1, but that protection is not current mainline behavior and is non-additive because the P0-B1 worktree predates P0-A-2.

The remaining estate is not a small tail. It includes direct client PDFs and UI, snapshot and audit export egress, direct physics APIs, operational workflow gates, cost/repairer selection, vehicle and cross-claim fraud feeds, and several risk or engineering surfaces whose permissible authority has not yet been decided. The correct next step is **not** to begin P0-A-3 or P0-B1c. The first task is a conflict-aware P0-B1 integration plan that preserves every P0-A-2 physics boundary, removes the known residual B1 fraud fallback, and then re-validates the combined package.

## Scope and classification

The scan covered production routers, report renderers, PDF and export producers, snapshot/replay/history/learning paths, pipeline authority sinks, analytics/admin APIs, and mounted client views. A consumer was included when it reads, returns, persists for later presentation, or acts on collision physics or fraud evidence. Collision physics includes crush/deformation, speed, delta-V, force, energy, deceleration, braking, physical impossibility, CGI, hidden/structural damage, causation, direction, and collision consistency. Fraud includes scores, probabilities, manipulation or AI-generation classifications, risk bands, alerts, fraud-derived approval/routing, and derived review recommendations.

The inventory uses the following status labels.

| Status                | Meaning                                                                                                      |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A2 covered**        | A collision-physics publication branch is confirmed fail-closed in merged main.                              |
| **B1 prospective**    | The paused P0-B1 overlay adds a fraud hold or redaction, but it is uncommitted and must be composed with A2. |
| **Open gap**          | A material consumer is active or callable without an A2/B1 boundary.                                         |
| **Owner decision**    | The source is a material authority or risk surface whose allowed use cannot be inferred safely from code.    |
| **Raw evidence only** | The source may preserve documented evidence but must not become a decision or publication authority.         |
| **Dormant/test only** | No audited non-test production caller was found; future wiring must still be gated.                          |

## What P0-A-2 has actually closed

P0-A-2 is a real and valuable boundary. It is confirmed for the shared physics presentation contract, Claims Intelligence, Forensic Decision Report, legacy report definitions, canonical assessment-PDF input, deterministic report narratives, and the causal-publication branch of Stage 7. In those paths, advisory or unavailable visual evidence cannot publish speed, crush, delta-V, force, energy, deceleration, braking, physical impossibility, CGI, hidden damage, structural/load-path, causation, direction, or collision-consistency output.

The package does not establish a general physics policy for browser UI, raw API read models, snapshots, audit export, physical workflow gates, cost recommendations, or engineering tools. Those are distinct consumers and must not be described as closed by PR #144.

## What P0-B1 would close if correctly integrated

P0-B1 is prospective. Its fraud hold usefully prevents score/level fabrication and returns manual review with evidence requirements at many central sinks: Stage 8, final decision, fast track, workflow routing, recovery, replay, learning/calibration, major analytics/portfolio/report outputs, selected vehicle passport reads, and the Agency Vehicle Verification report.

However, the overlay is based on a pre-A2 commit and changes overlapping report, PDF, narrative, snapshot, and router files. It cannot be merged as a replacement for mainline. In its present form, it can reintroduce the P0-A-2 physics disclosures in Claims Intelligence, Forensic Decision, report definitions, generic PDF, report-PDF, report narration, and claim-report routing. It also has one confirmed residual fraud disclosure/decision-context issue in the Forensic renderer’s numeric `fraudScoreAdjusted` fallback.

## Required sequence before any next package

1. **Do not start P0-A-3 or P0-B1c.** The inventory disproves the premise that they would be the final groups.
2. Prepare a **P0-B1 integration plan**, not implementation, that rebases the paused overlay onto `ee8b80e4`, retains every A2 helper call and regression, removes the `fraudScoreAdjusted` fallback, and defines combined P0 A2/B1 report, PDF, narrative, snapshot, and queue invariants.
3. Review the following authority decisions separately before code work: physical readiness/consistency/quality gates; physical/structural cost and repairer selection; incident and inspection reconciliation; audit export; engineering/underwriting/vendor-risk reports; automation policy configuration; and the status of human early-fraud escalation.
4. After B1 is safely integrated and validated, split the remaining bypasses by execution boundary rather than by the previous A3/B1c labels: browser export/UI, direct APIs and audit/snapshot egress, fraud feeds and vehicle timelines, and physical workflow/cost authority.
5. Keep E01-E06 as source-preserving evidence only. Do not use raw records, snapshots, learning stores, or dormant helpers to recreate a governing physics or fraud conclusion.

## Audit limits

This is a static, read-only source inventory. No tests, database access, staging access, production access, deployment, or source modification occurred. P0-B1 conclusions are prospective because the overlay was uncommitted. Absence of a non-test caller in the inspected graph does not prove that dynamic or future invocation is impossible. The 40 counts are canonical group counts, not path counts, and coverage/gap counts overlap because a mixed consumer can have an A2-safe physics branch and an unresolved fraud branch.
