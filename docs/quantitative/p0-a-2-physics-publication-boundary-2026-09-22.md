# P0-A-2 collision-physics publication boundary

**Date:** 22 September 2026
**Status:** Implementation, adversarial approval, and merge-readiness validation complete; GitHub review pending.
**Scope:** Collision-physics output disclosure only. This package extends P0-A. It does not change fraud authority, cost authority, model training, schema, seed data, staging, production, or deployment behavior.

## Purpose

P0-A established that the current visual geometry and raw Stage 6 crush inputs cannot govern collision physics. A subsequent report-surface review found that several legacy presentation paths could still display stored Physics Truth Layer, cross-validation, CGI, or forensic values. P0-A-2 closes that publication gap.

The package makes the report and export boundary fail closed. When the governing crush-depth eligibility predicate is not satisfied, current P0 behavior is always non-governing. Collision-physics values and conclusions are withheld from presentation even if historical records contain values that look complete.

## Enforced boundary

The shared `p0PhysicsPresentation.ts` boundary composes the existing `hasGoverningCrushDepthEligibility()` predicate from `quantitativeFieldGovernance.ts`. It never treats a stored Physics Truth Layer value, a legacy physics-analysis field, a cross-validation result, or a CGI result as authority by itself.

When eligibility is absent, the boundary emits a stable, actionable abstention record:

> **Collision Physics Withheld — Manual Review Required.** Current visual geometry, pixel-span interpretation, and raw crush observations are advisory or unavailable under the P0 evidence contract. The output identifies the missing authority and requires either a P1-qualified, source-bound governing collision measurement or a documented human engineering review that records its measurement basis and provenance.

The withholding rule covers raw or derived collision quantities and conclusions, including crush depth, delta-V, kinetic energy, force, deceleration, consensus or inferred speed, braking distance, physical-impossibility findings, CGI, hidden-damage and structural-load-path conclusions, collision causation, and collision-consistency verdicts. Descriptive, non-physical evidence remains available when it is independently supported.

## Shared projection contract

`server/reporting/p0PhysicsPresentation.ts` supplies four presentation-only functions. `hasP0A2CollisionPhysicsPublicationAuthority()` delegates eligibility to the P0-A contract. `buildP0A2CollisionPhysicsHold()` returns the structured manual-review record. `renderP0A2CollisionPhysicsAbstentionMarker()` renders the HTML form for reports and PDFs. `redactP0A2PhysicsReportPayload()` recursively strips collision-physics fields from generic report/PDF payloads and attaches the same structured abstention record.

The recursive redactor is deliberately separate from, and composable with, P0-B1's fraud redactor. Physics and fraud remain independent authorities, so future qualification of one cannot silently re-enable the other.

`projectP0A2DescriptivePhotoEvidence()` is the dedicated photo-boundary projection used by Claim Assessment, Claims Intelligence, and the Forensic Decision Report. It removes `suitableForCrushDepth`, `physicsExclusionReason`, and `directionContradiction` before each shared photo renderer receives the report object. The shared renderer remains unchanged for its existing non-P0 consumers; this package does not reinterpret stored photo metadata as live governing evidence.

## Adversarial-review remediation

The first independent adversarial review correctly blocked the package on two residual disclosure paths. First, `photoZonePanel()` could display stored `suitableForCrushDepth` as crush-depth eligible, surface a stored physics exclusion reason, and display a direction-versus-narrative conflict. The new explicit P0-safe photo projection removes those three fields at every in-scope Claim Assessment, Claims Intelligence, and Forensic Decision Report call site. The rich tenant-owned fixture now populates all three adversarial fields and proves that descriptive photo content remains visible while none of the collision-physics or direction-conclusion strings reaches any of the three reports.

Second, the Forensic Decision Report could display a stored `forensicAudit.riskSummary` whose fallback asserted an absence of physical-impossibility flags, plus `narrative.crossEngineAgreement` as a C1–C9 score. The risk-summary fallback is now an actionable collision-physics hold, and the date section is renamed to documentary accident-date reconciliation with only the independently sourced claim-form/police-report comparison retained. The fixture injects a distinctive physical-impossibility summary and a 92-point cross-engine agreement; both are absent from output while the documentary date reconciliation remains visible.

## Second adversarial-review remediation

A fresh review found that the Forensic Decision Report still projected `physics_analysis.damageZones`, visual severity, impact direction, and a classified-zone-versus-photo coherence verdict. It also still displayed forensic-audit scores, validation checks, issue text, and next steps through a lexical filter. That was not source-bound authorization, because a new or indirect physics conclusion could bypass a finite list of phrases. The repair removes those projections rather than filtering them. The FDR now replaces the zone/coherence and forensic-physics validation panels with the shared actionable hold. It preserves the shared descriptive photo projection, tenant-scoped registration-history count, cost evidence, policy evidence, workflow, and approval information. Its visible fraud category breakdown now excludes the physical-consistency, scenario-intelligence, and photo-forensics categories; it preserves only the non-physics financial, documentation, and entity categories.

The same review found that the legacy `report-narrative-generator.ts` still had live free-form LLM prompts capable of turning an incident narrative or damage text into a collision conclusion. The P0-A-2 path now has no LLM invocation. It builds all eight `ReportNarrative` fields deterministically and inserts the common hold wherever collision content could be inferred. The new regression mocks an adversarial LLM response and injects adversarial incident, damage, and raw physics text. It proves no LLM call occurs and none of the distinctive collision speed, physical-impossibility, hidden-damage, or coherence strings enters the published narrative.

## Final adversarial-review remediation

The final fresh review found that the generic legacy PDF renderer still accepted arbitrary caller-controlled objects. Recursive key redaction could not establish the provenance of ordinary strings, component names, reasoning, zones, or unknown nested branches, so collision conclusions could be supplied in an otherwise retained field. `generateAssessmentReportHTML()` now recognises only a private marker attached by `toAssessmentPdfCanonicalInput()` after tenant-scoped canonical resolution. Direct legacy input uses a minimal empty projection; it cannot publish any caller content. The canonical adapter intentionally excludes free-form incident and damage narratives, component names, recommendation reasoning, normalised zones, cross-validation output, and arbitrary branches. It retains canonical vehicle identifiers and the independently supported cost field, and every resulting PDF includes the shared actionable collision-physics hold. New regressions inject collision aliases into all previously retained-looking fields and prove none reaches output.

The same review found that all three report tiers could still use legacy repair/cost JSON or Claim Truth free-text to publish structural classifications, component-damage conclusions, structural-review requirements, or collision-derived review triggers. Claim Assessment, Claims Intelligence, and the Forensic Decision Report now pass no `extractExplicitStructuralReviewEvidence()` result into their shared cost summaries. Claims Intelligence and the FDR no longer derive `criticalStructural`, publish structural-gap prose, or display raw reconciliation notes. FDR review context is reconstructed only from non-physics numeric fraud and data-completeness factors; it does not interpolate Claim Truth trigger text. Claim Assessment and the legacy forensic report likewise no longer render stored fraud-indicator text, because it has no source-bound presentation contract and can contain collision-derived prose. The rich tenant-scoped fixture now supplies distinctive structural-gap, structural-review, reconciliation, fraud-indicator, and Claim Truth collision aliases and asserts that all three reports retain their non-physics claim, photo, quote, cost, workflow, and approval content while omitting every alias.

The next read-only review found one further Claim Assessment bypass: its Final Recommendation rendered the `decision_authority_json` summary, or stringified the entire legacy object when no summary existed. This was removed rather than filtered. The panel now has a fixed statement built only from the canonical report-decision status and the independently governed cost and workflow boundary; it repeats that collision conclusions remain withheld. The rich fixture injects both a legacy decision-authority speed claim and a causation claim, and verifies their absence from Claim Assessment, Claims Intelligence, and the Forensic Decision Report output.

## Deliberate non-goals

P0-A-2 does not qualify visual measurements, invent a governing physics value, alter physical calculations, rewrite historical records, or suppress independently supported descriptive evidence. It also does not replace P0-B1’s fraud redaction work. The package only prevents advisory or unavailable collision-physics sources from being published as if they were authoritative.
