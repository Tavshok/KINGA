# Review-Only Proposal: P0 Authority-Policy Decisions

**Status:** **Decision-ready policy proposal only.** This document makes no implementation, merge, rebase, source-edit, data-change, migration, deployment, or runtime-approval decision. It is intended to let owners classify authority before any later package is designed.

## Policy premise

P0 must distinguish three different things. **Raw evidence** is a preserved source record or observation. **Documented human-reviewed evidence** is a claim- and tenant-bound record that identifies the reviewer, source or measurement basis, provenance, date, scope, and human disposition. **Automated authority** is a separately authorized, source-bound rule that may cause a publication, score, recommendation, workflow transition, routing, cost/repairer effect, approval/denial, or other action.

Preserving an item does not authorize it. A snapshot hash, an access check, a model confidence score, a historic value, a fallback, a human note, or a visible UI label is not automated authority. P0-A-2 governs certain collision-physics publication paths; it does not decide the remaining workflow, cost, audit, browser, API, engineering, or fraud authority surfaces. The paused P0-B1 overlay is prospective and must not be treated as current behavior. [1] [2] [3] [4]

> **Common P0 rule:** Where authority is unresolved, retain authorized evidence for provenance and human review, but return an explicit, nonnumeric manual-review/withheld state. That state is neither a benign pass nor an adverse finding.

## 1. Physical readiness, consistency, quality, and pre-analysis gates (M20)

**Decision statement.** Decide whether current physical or structural outputs may govern readiness, consistency, quality, review severity, pre-analysis progression, or final automated decision behavior in `server/pipeline-v2/decisionReadinessEngine.ts`, `server/pipeline-v2/crossStageConsistencyEngine.ts`, `server/pipeline-v2/claimQualityScorer.ts`, `server/pipeline-v2/pipelineGateController.ts`, `server/pipeline-v2/stage-4-validation.ts`, `server/pipeline-v2/stage-10-report.ts`, `server/pipeline-v2/orchestrator.ts`, `server/pipeline-v2/claimsDecisionAuthority.ts`, and `server/routers/inspections.ts`.

**Exact recommended default.** **At P0, no current physical or structural output may authorize a favorable readiness/quality result, an automated APPROVE/REJECT, routing, certification, cost/repairer effect, or report-generation transition. Every such use resolves to an actionable manual-review hold.**

**Current bounded treatment.** Raw photos, documents, descriptive damage/component observations, provenance, and documentary incident facts may remain reviewer-visible. A qualified human may document an engineering review with measurement basis and provenance for a case-specific human decision. Stage 4 and Stage 10 may preserve non-authoritative completeness prompts, review issues, degradation reasons, and audit records. Those channels cannot convert physics execution, confidence, consistency, structural status, or a client-supplied value into authority. [3] [4]

**Rationale.** P0-A-2’s physics predicate deliberately fails closed for current visual geometry and raw crush evidence. Yet current readiness, consistency, and quality mechanisms may turn physics execution, low-confidence warnings, consistency flags, or structural content into a positive readiness/quality or downstream decision signal. Those inconsistent semantics are not a source-bound governing measurement. Evidence preservation therefore supports review but cannot justify automation. [3] [4]

**Future reopening condition.** Reopen automated physical authority only through an owner-approved P1-or-later policy that names the governing source, claim linkage, purpose-specific consumers, schema/provenance/freshness controls, live-source rebinding, positive eligibility predicate, and explicit failed-gate outcome. A documented qualified engineering review can reopen only the defined **human-review** workflow after basis, calibration/provenance, authorization, and responsibility are recorded; it does not create automated authority. [3] [4]

**Implementation consequence.** A later authorized package would put the hold before Stage 4 interpretation, Stage 10 readiness/consistency/quality output, orchestration effects, final decision, and inspection report-generation transition. It would preserve authorization checks and reviewer-visible evidence while blocking favorable or adverse automated effects from raw, stale, forged, missing, or client-supplied physical data.

**Validation evidence required.** Future tests must show that Stage 6/VGE/VGR values, `physicsExecuted`, low confidence, stale or malformed eligibility, and frontend-supplied reconciliation data cannot produce a favorable readiness/quality grade or authority. Ordering tests must prove authorization precedes withholding where necessary, and the hold precedes a decision, route, cost effect, report release, or inspection transition. Existing A2/B1 results are baseline evidence only; no runtime validation is asserted here. [2] [3] [4]

**Owner choices.** Owners may adopt the proposed full abstention; permit only a narrowly documented human engineering-review action; or defer the decision and leave all affected consumers held. They should not select an automated option without the P1 policy prerequisites.

[2] [3] [4]

**Owner choices.** Owners may adopt default abstention; permit a provenance-bearing **human-only** cost/repair/timing disposition; or establish a future automated authority program with the stated P1 policy. They may not infer permission from current code or a B1 fraud hold.

**Validation evidence required.** Future tests must cover adversarial LLM “pass” output and heuristic fallback; absent/empty/zero/NOT_AVAILABLE pipeline inputs; within- and over-threshold comparisons; stale legacy records; and documented human review. Every P0 case must show no `physicsReconciled=1`, timestamp, success note, `report_generation`, Physics Truth mutation, or green report/passport display. [2] [3]

**Owner choices.** Owners may adopt evidence-only treatment; permit an explicitly scoped documented-human-review resolution; or create a later qualified automated state-transition policy. Historical remediation and schema changes remain separate decisions.

## 4. Report snapshots, interactive/PDF/email delivery, and governed audit export (M07 and M30)

Issue, deliver, or externally export only allow-listed descriptive or documented human-reviewed evidence plus explicit A2 physics and B1 fraud holds. **Future reopening condition.** Physics publication/export requires the A2/P1 governing measurement or the documented qualified engineering-review route. Fraud automation requires a later positive, source-bound fraud authority. Any external audit export additionally needs an owner-approved recipient, purpose, allow-list, retention, authorization, provenance, human-review, and composed A2/B1 eligibility policy. [3] [4]

**Implementation consequence.** A later authorized design would apply the composite projection at snapshot ingress and every egress: direct generation, snapshot write/read, PDF regeneration, narrative/visualization input, interactive response, email initiation, download, and audit assembly/hashing. A legacy raw snapshot remains a restricted provenance store, not a public read model. Clients must render explicit holds, and legacy PDF links cannot bypass the safe artifact. [2] [3]

**Validation evidence required.** Future source and behavior tests must inject nested aliases, legacy snapshot JSON, raw physics/fraud fields, free text, and client-cache-like payloads. They must prove no legacy raw snapshot is returned, becomes PDF input, or becomes emailable. Audit tests must cover verbatim and reconstructed export paths, authorization ordering, deterministic withheld behavior before raw payload assembly, and later allow-list/hash behavior only after owner approval. [2] [3]

**Owner choices.** Owners may (a) hold all external export under P0, (b) authorize a narrow human-reviewed documentary export with recipient/purpose controls, or (c) authorize a later structured export program. They should not rely on hash, snapshot finality, or tenant access as authority.
